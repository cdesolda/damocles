## 📌 Overview  

**DAMOCLES** aims to develop an integrated framework for the **cyber defense of Italian Public Administrations** against security incidents caused by human error.  

The project focuses on two main pillars:  

- **Human Vulnerability Assessment (HVA)** – activities for prevention, detection, and simulation to identify vulnerabilities related to users’ behavior, knowledge, and habits.  
  - *Prevention*: through questionnaires identifying risky behaviors and evaluating their severity  
  - *Detection*: through “safe” simulated attacks (e.g., phishing) within PAs to observe real reactions  
  - *Simulation*: through Digital Twins of users, reproducing their behavior to predict vulnerabilities  

- **Human Vulnerability Mitigation (HVM)** – based on HVA results, design of **personalized training strategies** to reduce human vulnerabilities. Examples include podcasts, video tutorials, role-play simulations of attacks, and contextual in-app messages.  

The project started on **November 30, 2023**, and has a duration of 24 months.  
Funded by the **European Union – NextGenerationEU**, within the **Italian PRIN PNRR 2022** program.
